CAMIPAY

Making an unknown payments brand feel safe enough to ask for a quote.

A social media strategy sample for Camipay, a Cameroon-based service that lets businesses pay foreign suppliers by depositing FCFA at a counter.

The challenge

The offer was clear on the website. On social media it barely existed, in a category where the first question is always “is this a scam?”.

10 followers, 2 postsCamipay’s Facebook page when I audited it, with comments off on one post.
One tagline, no proofPosts carried hashtags and a slogan, but no steps, no price example, no reason to trust.
Pain already namedDelays, blocked operations and hidden fees: the site speaks them well, social did not.

Blue Ocean positioning

Wise and Taptap Send compete on lifestyle humour, giveaways and promo codes. I chose the space they leave empty for Cameroonian importers.

For Cameroonian businesses that pay suppliers abroad, Camipay is the counter-to-supplier payment with proof: deposit FCFA, see the exact price first, and follow your payment until the supplier has it.
